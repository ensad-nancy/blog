# A Message To Myself 
Why do you make everything so hard for yourself? Basically, all you’d need to do is write down your tasks, structure them, put time slots for completing them into your calendar, and stick to those appointments. Planning really isn’t a big deal, but for you it seems to be one of the biggest hurdles.

You watch videos about how people plan and structure their everyday lives, how they optimize themselves and grow. You think: That’s all really simple, I can do that too. But before you start putting anything into practice, you look for another video that might offer even more or better tips. And then you scroll through your feed for hours—looking for even more content that keeps you away from real life and continues to steal your time.
Another video found, started—again the same tips—didn’t finish it and turned it off. Back to searching for something new. Your mind tells you that this isn’t right, that you should rather get creative yourself and start creating things. But unfortunately, you can’t manage to pull yourself away. Before you know it, several hours have passed again, and nothing you watched has stuck.

I believe you're desperately searching for balance from the constant challenges and obligations that are placed on you every day or that you burden yourself with. What if social media didn’t exist? What would you do then? No matter what it would be— it would definitely be closer to reality and more calming than this digital frenzy.
Well, I have to admit: your university tasks and your side job demand a lot of energy from you. But with more focused planning, that could be changed. You constantly have things on your mind that worry you because you’re afraid you won’t finish in time. Oh shit… tomorrow is the deadline again and I haven’t done anything—what now?

It’s important to take time for yourself and sometimes simply do nothing. But the things you’ve committed to still need to be done. In the end, you’ll feel more confident and productive—maybe even smarter. Just finally start turning your thoughts into reality and enjoy life more. Be grateful for all your opportunities and don’t let them sink into the sand. Others would be glad to be in your position.

*L.D.*
