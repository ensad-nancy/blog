# An Evening in My Life

Saturday, December 6th, 2025 – St. Nicholas Day, and Nancy was buzzing with energy. I had kindly been invited by someone from the university to an apéro. At the same time, the Saint Nicholas Parade was taking place in the city. We met at Place Stanislas to watch the parade’s finale. Extravagantly decorated floats, performers, cheerful children, musicians, and dancers passed by us. The highlight was a float that looked like King Kong — it lit up and could even move. A DJ played modern Christmas music the whole time, and a host announced the different participants of the parade.

Finally, the moment had come: St. Nicholas arrived at Place Stanislas. He stepped out of his vehicle and walked up the stairs of the hotel to speak to the visitors from the balcony. His speech was mainly addressed to the children. After his address, an impressive fireworks display lit up the sky above the hotel. It was colorful, bright, and loud — absolutely beautiful, especially since I hadn’t seen a real fireworks show in a long time.

After the show, we headed to the apartment. There were drinks, snacks, music, and great conversations. We sang and danced. The evening was a complete success.
